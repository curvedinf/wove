# Documentation references

Optional inspiration, inspected 2026-10-12:

- [uv](https://github.com/astral-sh/uv#readme): a specific opening promise, visible evidence, and concrete usage examples.
- [Ollama](https://github.com/ollama/ollama#readme): a brief outcome-oriented opening and direct routes to getting started.
- [Supabase's published redesign](https://supabase.com/blog/new-supabase-docs-built-with-nextjs): coordinated icons, panels, cards, and product/docs styling. A historical design reference, not a claim about the current layout.
- [Stripe Checkout](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/checkout): product previews and demo access alongside implementation choices, letting readers inspect the experience before integrating.
- [Starlight cards](https://starlight.astro.build/components/cards/) and [code panels](https://starlight.astro.build/components/code/): recognizable navigation choices and examples with filenames, highlighting, and copy controls.

Adapt the principles to the audience and rendering platform. Trust benefits are design judgments; popularity or visual polish does not establish adoption gains or product reliability.
